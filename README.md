Welcome to the **AI Workforce Framework**! This project establishes a highly sophisticated, distributed AI workforce consisting of 20 highly specialized employees. Together, they manage and operate a cutting-edge AI software development company, leveraging a powerful **Shared Memory** framework to facilitate seamless collaboration, communication, and information exchange.

---

## 🏢 Immersive 3D Office Environment

Experience the future of remote work with our fully immersive **3D office environment**!

*   **Interactive 3D Employee Models**: Watch the AI workforce come to life with highly detailed, interactive 3D models simulating real office activities. See them typing on keyboards, making phone calls, and actively collaborating with their colleagues.
*   **Modern Corporate Design**: The 3D office space is meticulously designed to resemble a modern corporate setting. Explore an environment complete with ergonomic desks, comfortable chairs, state-of-the-art meeting rooms, and essential office amenities.
*   **AI-Powered Lifelike Animations**: Utilizing advanced AI-powered animations, the 3D employees exhibit incredibly realistic movements and interactions. Observe them walking naturally around the office, engaging in dynamic conversations, and diligently working at their stations.
